# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Command Governor welcomes contributors and users of every background and
experience level. Participate with respect, patience, and professional care.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- giving actionable feedback and accepting correction;
- respecting privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of experience;
- making room for others to contribute; and
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm where possible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review
threads, community events, and public interactions made on behalf of the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's platform reporting tools for abusive content. For a project-level
concern, contact a maintainer privately using contact information published on
their GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue
requesting private contact without describing sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently exclude participants whose conduct harms the community. Reports
will be handled as privately and consistently as circumstances permit. Anyone
with a conflict of interest should recuse themselves from an enforcement
decision.
